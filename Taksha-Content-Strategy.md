# Taksha Content Strategy & Copywriting Guidelines

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Brand:** Taksha
**Voice:** Confident, Concise, Inspiring, Precise

---

## 1. Core Principles of Taksha Copy
1. **Show, Don't Tell:** Avoid overused adjectives like "innovative" or "cutting-edge." Describe *what* we built and *how* it solves the problem.
2. **Ruthless Brevity:** Cut the fluff. Respect the user's time.
3. **Radical Transparency:** No fabricated metrics, fake clients, or exaggerated claims.
4. **Action-Oriented:** Write to inspire momentum. Use strong, active verbs.

## 2. Core Website Copy

### 2.1 Homepage Copy
- **Hero Headline:** Crafting Digital Excellence.
- **Hero Subheadline:** We are a premium digital craft studio merging meticulous UI/UX design, rigorous frontend engineering, and AI automation to build the extraordinary.
- **Value Prop Sections:** Highlight our core triads: Design, Engineering, AI. Keep descriptions under 2 sentences per pillar.

### 2.2 About Page Copy
- **The Hook:** We don't just build websites; we engineer digital artifacts.
- **The Story:** Explain the origin of "Takṣ" (to carve/craft with precision). Emphasize our rejection of the "agency factory model" in favor of a boutique, high-craft studio approach.
- **The Stance:** Include a transparent manifesto stating our refusal to use fake case studies or inflated metrics.

### 2.3 Contact Page Copy
- **Headline:** Let's build something meaningful.
- **Subheadline:** Whether you have a clear blueprint or just a visionary idea, we are ready to craft it into reality.
- **Expectation Setting:** "We reply to all serious inquiries within 24 hours. No sales teams, just engineers and designers."

## 3. Page Strategies

### 3.1 Landing Pages (Campaigns)
- **Structure:** 
  1. High-impact Hero (Problem/Solution).
  2. Social Proof (Real work, not fake logos).
  3. The Process (How we solve the problem).
  4. The Outcome (What they get).
  5. Clear, singular CTA.
- **Rule:** Remove all distracting navigation links. The goal is a single conversion action.

### 3.2 Service Pages
- **Tone:** Educational and authoritative.
- **Structure:** Define the service (e.g., Frontend Engineering), detail the tech stack (React, Next.js, WebGL), explain the Taksha methodology, and show 1-2 highly relevant portfolio pieces.

### 3.3 Case Study Structure
Every piece of work must follow the **CRAFT** framework:
- **C**ontext: What was the client's original problem?
- **R**esearch: What did our discovery phase reveal?
- **A**pproach: How did we design/architect the solution?
- **F**eatures: Deep-dive into specific engineering or design details (e.g., "How we optimized the WebGL render loop").
- **T**akeaways: Real, honest reflections on what we learned and built. No fabricated ROI percentages.

### 3.4 Blog Strategy ("The Journal")
- **Focus:** Thought leadership on frontend architecture, UI/UX philosophy, and agency transparency.
- **Format:** Long-form technical tutorials (e.g., "Advanced GSAP Scroll Triggers"), opinion pieces on design trends, and transparent studio updates.
- **Rule:** Zero AI-generated filler content. If we don't have something valuable to say, we don't publish.

## 5. Microcopy & System Messages

### 5.1 Error Messages (404s, Form Errors)
- **404 Page:** "404 - Void Reached. The page you are looking for doesn't exist, or it has been archived. [Return to Base]"
- **Form Error:** "Something went wrong. Please check the highlighted fields and try again." (Keep it helpful, not robotic).
- **Network Error:** "Connection lost. We're having trouble reaching the server. Please check your internet connection."

### 5.2 Success States & Notifications
- **Form Success:** "Message received. We'll review your inquiry and an engineer will be in touch within 24 hours."
- **Action Success (Toast):** "Changes saved successfully." (Short, confirming, disappears quickly).

### 5.3 Empty States
- **Portfolio (if filtering yields no results):** "No projects found for this filter. Try adjusting your search criteria to see more of our work."
- **General Empty:** Provide a clear "Next Step" action rather than just a dead end.

## 7. SEO Content Guidelines

- **Meta Titles:** `[Page Name] | Taksha — Digital Craft Studio` (Keep under 60 characters).
- **Meta Descriptions:** Start with a strong action verb. Include primary keywords naturally. (e.g., "Partner with Taksha to engineer premium digital experiences. We specialize in React, UI/UX design, and AI automation.") Keep under 155 characters.
- **Alt Text:** Describe the image accurately for screen readers. Never keyword-stuff alt attributes.
- **URL Slugs:** Short, hyphenated, lowercase (e.g., `/services/frontend-engineering`).
