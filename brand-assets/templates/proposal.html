01. Executive Summary

The Objective

Based on our discovery conversations, [Company Name] is seeking to completely overhaul its digital presence. The current platform suffers from severe technical debt, outdated branding, and a user experience that creates friction during the conversion funnel.

Taksha will partner with your team to design and develop a premium, scalable web platform. Our goal is not just to refresh the aesthetics, but to rebuild the foundation using modern web architecture (React/Vite), ensuring blazing-fast performance, strict accessibility compliance, and a design language that positions you as an industry leader.

02. Our Approach

How We Build

Strategy First

We don't design in a vacuum. Every interface decision is backed by user research and your business goals.

Premium Craft

We obsess over typography, spacing, and micro-interactions. The result is an experience that feels undeniably high-end.

Engineering Rigor

Beautiful design means nothing if it's slow. We target 100/100 Lighthouse scores on every build.

Transparent Process

No black boxes. You will have direct access to our Figma files and staging environments throughout the project.

03. Scope of Work

Project Phases

Phase 1

Discovery & Brand Alignment

  • Stakeholder interviews and competitor analysis
  • Audience persona development
  • Brand identity refinement (Typography, Color, Voice)
  • Information Architecture (IA) and Sitemap

Phase 2

UI/UX Design

  • Low-fidelity wireframes for core user flows
  • High-fidelity interface design in Figma
  • Interactive prototyping for stakeholder review
  • Creation of a scalable Design System

Phase 3

Frontend Engineering

  • Setup modern React/Vite architecture
  • Pixel-perfect translation of Figma designs to code
  • Implementation of GSAP/Framer Motion animations
  • Performance optimization & WCAG AA Accessibility audit
