Project Overview

Based on our discovery calls, we understand that you are looking to elevate your digital presence through a complete brand refresh and a high-performance web application. Taksha will provide end-to-end design and engineering to achieve these goals.

The Challenge

Your current platform lacks the premium aesthetic required to attract enterprise clients, and the frontend architecture is suffering from slow load times and accessibility issues.

Our Approach

1. Brand Identity

Developing a scalable visual system including typography, color tokens, and logo refinements that reflect a premium market position.

2. UI/UX Design

Creating high-fidelity, interactive prototypes in Figma focusing on conversion-optimized user flows and WCAG AA accessibility.

3. Frontend Engineering

Building the approved designs using React, Vite, and GSAP for fluid, performant animations targeting 95+ Lighthouse scores.
